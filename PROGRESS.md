## Known Deviations from SRS

**Rooms Phase 6's fourth panel is live presence, not a sessions-backed activity log (2026-07-25).** The rooms build plan asks for an "Activity log" backed by a `room_sessions(joined_at, left_at)` table plus a background job closing stale rows. That contradicts `retry_srs.md` line 301 — "No session timestamps or attendance history are kept" — and CLAUDE.md's room rules. The SRS's actual requirements here, FR-ROOM-07/08, describe *live presence*: who currently holds a connection to the room. Built accordingly: a presence panel (member roster + "Active now" from live actors + a visitors section), no sessions table, no closer job. This deviates from the **build plan** in order to comply with the **SRS**, which outranks it.
