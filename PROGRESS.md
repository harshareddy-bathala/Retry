## Current State

**Rooms R6 — beta polish (2026-07-27).** The track that turns "the rooms work" into "the rooms are shippable to a cohort". Four commits.

*Correctness.* One structural defect behind several symptoms: the EventBus broadcasts but never replays, so anything subscribing late holds a stale view — which is why the presence strip could sit on "RECONNECTING…" while the world was plainly working. Socket status is now READ from the socket (`getStatus`/`subscribe`, the pattern `av-store.ts` already used). A half-open socket read as a healthy world, so there is now an application-level `ping`/`pong` (RFC 6455's own ping frame is unreachable from a page) with four unanswered beats treated as death. Build-plan Phase 8.1 is finished: remote avatars freeze and dim rather than vanishing, local movement keeps working, a non-blocking banner says so, and after five attempts the client stops and offers **Rejoin** — retrying forever is worse than stopping. The composited-character cache never evicted (~393 KB each, cleared only on scene shutdown) and is now LRU-capped at 40 with the live cast pinned. **The seventh public room could not be created at all**: every Commons door slot was taken and creation 409'd. A door is a shortcut into a room, not the room's licence to exist — public rooms are now created doorless when the wall is full, listed and enterable from the Rooms tab, and pick up a door the moment one frees. `NO_FREE_DOOR_SLOT` is gone from the error codes.

*Maps.* Rendered all five rooms and looked at them, which had not been done side by side before. Three carried defects nobody had seen. The Commons and the conference room were laid on floor blocks that look flat in a sheet preview and tile into a lattice of offset rectangles across a room (the 3×2 unit has a lit edge and the seeder repeats the unit) — both are now single interior tiles. The lounge's herringbone tiled correctly and was still wrong: at room scale it was the loudest thing in the building. `tableRound` pointed at a **crate stacked on a sofa arm** and had been rendering in four rooms as an unidentifiable slab. Conference seating was lattice-backed chairs picked without regard to facing, so half had their backs where the sitter's face should be. The Commons went from 28×12 with six doors and a bare middle to **40×16 with twelve doors** and a seating island in the centre — twelve at a three-tile pitch, not sixteen, because the door *sprite* is one tile wide and sixteen reads as one continuous row of doors with colliding plaques. Re-authoring moved the slots, which would have stranded every existing public room on coordinates the room server maps BY COORDINATE; the API now reconciles at boot, so this is self-healing rather than a step somebody has to remember. The classroom's perfect 3×3 desk grid is staggered and wall decoration is scattered from a seeded PRNG.

*Gather feel.* Emotes (eight, from the pack's thought-bubble atlas, which had been catalogued and unused since the art landed — a 10×10 grid whose columns are frames, so the build crops chosen pairs into a strip); the typing indicator the last handover deliberately deferred, broadcast to the room **channel** so one event drives a bubble over an avatar and "Ana is typing…" in a Workspace that has no avatar; **proximity speech** — `chat` gains `scope: room|nearby`, where nearby is delivered only to the peers the proximity engine already says are close, appears over the speaker's head for six seconds, and is **never persisted**. It reuses the same zone state that drives the audio bubbles, so what you can hear and what you can read agree by construction. Sitting is deliberately *not* protocol: a seat is a map interactable and sitting sends an ordinary `move`, so sittable chairs carry no collision and the validator rejects a seat on a collision tile (it caught one on its first run). Plus a minimap drawn from the collision grid, click-a-name-to-pan, and a Say bar on Enter so speech works in the Commons, which has no chat panel.

*Before students.* The desktop gate (build plan Phase 8.4, SRS §10) — under 1024px or a coarse pointer gets a sentence and a link to the Workspace instead of a canvas with no keyboard. The accessibility story is now deliberate rather than accidental: the canvas says what it is and names the Workspace as the equivalent, in the aria label and the gate copy.

**Rooms AV migrated from Daily.co to self-hosted LiveKit (2026-07-25, ADR-012).** The `AvProvider` seam built in Phase 5 did its job: the swap touched the provider, the `avToken` message shape, and the client manager, and nothing else. Server (`av/livekit.ts`): a grant is now **pure local JWT signing** — LiveKit rooms are implicit, so the Daily room-creation cache and its failure modes are gone and no third-party call sits in the room-entry path. **Rooms R3 — room lifecycle & membership complete locally (2026-07-25).** The biggest functional gap in the whole rooms track: until now a private room was a room of one forever, because no invite, leave, transfer, rename or delete endpoint existed. Migration `0003` adds `room_invites` (unique index on `(room_id, invitee_id) WHERE status = 'pending'` — one live invite per person, re-inviting after a decline still allowed), a generic `notifications` table (`kind` is text, not an enum, so posts and grading can add kinds without an `ALTER TYPE` Drizzle cannot run inside a migration transaction), `rooms.last_activity_at` and `room_members.presence_seen_at`. API: `PATCH`/`DELETE /rooms/:id`, `POST /rooms/:id/invites`, `GET /invites`, `POST /invites/:id/accept|decline`, `DELETE /rooms/:id/members/:userId`, `POST /rooms/:id/transfer`, `GET /rooms/:id`, plus `GET/POST /notifications`; `GET /rooms` now carries member counts, last activity and who is in the live space right now. Decisions worth naming: a **decline notifies nobody**, so saying no costs a student nothing socially; an owner who leaves promotes the longest-standing member, and a **sole owner is refused** with "delete the room instead" rather than having their chat and board destroyed by a button that does not say delete; a room turning private evicts visitors but keeps members, or privacy-by-absence would be cosmetic. **Cross-process eviction** (`POST /internal/evict`, `POST /internal/doors-changed` on the room server, shared-secret guarded, private network only) is what makes a removal take effect while someone is standing in the room: they are told `evicted` and walked to the Commons **on the same socket**, with their knock grant revoked. Presence is a 20 s heartbeat into one mutable cell, NULLed on socket close — no sessions table, no attendance history. Web: a `/rooms/:id` detail page (roster with live presence dots, invite by college email, transfer, leave, delete behind a type-the-name confirmation) that is also the shell R4 fills in, and a notification bell in the app shell polling invites + feed once a minute. One real bug found by driving it: **every bodyless POST was 400ing** — the API client always sent `content-type: application/json`, and Fastify rejects an empty body with a JSON content-type, so accepting an invite failed in the browser while passing in tests (`app.inject` sends no content-type). 27 new tests (14 API integration, 13 room-server WS; 140 across the workspace). Verified by a two-browser headless-Edge drive, 19/19: A creates a private room → invites B by email → B's bell badges → B accepts → B walks into a room they could not previously reach → A watches B's presence dot light up → A removes B → B receives `evicted`, lands in the Commons on an unbroken socket with a plain-English banner, and the room disappears from their list → A deletes the room behind the typed confirmation.

**Rooms R4 — Workspace view complete locally (2026-07-25).** A room used to be useful only while everyone was simultaneously walking around a 2D map; the other half of what the SRS calls a room did not exist. `/rooms/:id` is now the Workspace (FR-ROOM-09): project context header (stage, domain tag), Project Blueprint (three optional free-text questions, FR-ROOM-11), Build Journey timeline (FR-ROOM-17), and the chat/board/whiteboard panels — no canvas, no avatar. **The architectural key is a `watch` mode on the existing socket**: `{t:'watch', roomId}` subscribes a member to a room's channel with no actor, no proximity and no AV, so the Workspace reuses the Live Space's panel components *unchanged* and both views stay in step over one transport (FR-ROOM-33/FR-ROOM-10). `RoomHub.broadcast` stays the single fan-out point and now reaches watchers too — but only for events a page without a canvas can use; movement and proximity, the highest-volume messages on the wire, stop at the map boundary. Migration `0004` adds `rooms.project_stage`/`domain_tag`, `room_blueprint`, `room_blueprint_edits` (FR-ROOM-12's edit log — the team's own reference, never faculty-visible) and `room_journey_entries`. Journey entries are server-generated only: room creation, a field's **first-ever** answer, and stage changes; the weekly Done count is computed from the board at read time rather than stored, so a card moving out of Done stops being counted. Two things fell out of building it: watching a room must not be presence (opening the page never puts your avatar in the map), and the "one presence per user" supersede rule now only closes sessions **standing on a map**, because a Workspace open in a second tab is not a competing avatar. One real bug found by driving it: chat sent from the Workspace was attributed to "Anonymous" — `watch` carried no display name, unlike `join`. Also stripped the dev scaffolding from the Live Space (the EventBus log panel and the "phase 4 · multi-map" caption were the first thing a tester saw). 12 new WS tests (84 in room-server; 152 across the workspace). Drive 15/15: A works in the Workspace while B walks around the same room — blueprint answer, stage change, chat both ways, a card added from the Workspace, B's arrival showing up as presence, and zero movement frames reaching the Workspace.

**Rooms R5 — real art, real characters (2026-07-26).** The world was programmer-placeholder until now: four flat tiles and one grey avatar. It is now drawn. `packages/maps/art/` holds a dependency-free pixel toolkit (a ~60-line PNG encoder over Node's zlib, a blending canvas, a deterministic PRNG) and the art itself as **code** — one drawing function per tile, one parametric character generator — so a change to the art direction is a reviewable diff rather than an opaque binary. 38 tiles: plank floors with grain and staggered joints, 2.5D walls (dark top face, lit front face, skirting), a sand-and-copper rug, desks with lit monitors, chairs, sofas, tables, plants, shelves, a lamp, a window, a whiteboard that spans two tiles without a seam, doors that read as openings (lintel shadow, copper architrave, boarded leaf), and a lit exit threshold for walls seen from above. Both maps were re-authored from an ASCII layout in `scripts/author-maps.ts` — a room you can read in the source instead of 300 numbers per layer — and still emit ordinary Tiled JSON that opens in Tiled. **Six avatars chosen by personality** (FR-ROOM-24, a P1 requirement never built): Maker, Planner, Night Owl, Explorer, Tinkerer, Connector, each with a blurb a student actually picks on, a distinct silhouette (headphones, bun, beanie, backpack, curls, scarf) and a 4-direction 4-frame walk cycle. The picker appears on first entry to a room and never again, because the **server** answers "have you chosen here before?" — a student may have chosen months ago on another device. `msg.sprite` is no longer taken raw from the wire: it is whitelisted against the preset list, which closes a hole where any client could walk around as any string. Migration `0005` stores the choice per member per room as the preset KEY, not an index, so retiring a preset never silently reassigns everyone's character. Three art passes were thrown away en route and the reasons are in the code: per-board tone made the floor read as brickwork, a centred bun read as a bowler hat, and arced blobs read as rabbit ears. 152 tests green; drive 5/5 — world loads, picker offers all six with blurbs, the choice persists, and an invented sprite key is refused.

## Known Deviations from SRS

**Rooms Phase 6's fourth panel is live presence, not a sessions-backed activity log (2026-07-25).** The rooms build plan asks for an "Activity log" backed by a `room_sessions(joined_at, left_at)` table plus a background job closing stale rows. That contradicts `retry_srs.md` line 301 — "No session timestamps or attendance history are kept" — and CLAUDE.md's room rules. The SRS's actual requirements here, FR-ROOM-07/08, describe *live presence*: who currently holds a connection to the room. Built accordingly: a presence panel (member roster + "Active now" from live actors + a visitors section), no sessions table, no closer job. This deviates from the **build plan** in order to comply with the **SRS**, which outranks it.

**Rooms AV uses self-hosted LiveKit, not Daily.co (2026-07-25).** SRS FR-ROOM-29 names Daily.co explicitly. At 4000–5000 students Daily's per-participant-minute pricing is the one unbounded variable cost against a ~$39/month infrastructure budget, and self-hosting keeps student media on infrastructure the college controls. The half of FR-ROOM-29 that carries the actual engineering intent — "no custom WebRTC implementation shall be built" — is honoured: LiveKit is an established open-source SFU, not a bespoke stack. See `DECISIONS.md` ADR-012.

**Known flake:** the room-server WS suite occasionally fails a timing assertion when the whole
workspace test run happens alongside browser drives on the same machine — its `until()` helpers use
real timers with a 3 s budget. Seen once, not reproducible in isolation. Test stability belongs to
R6's hardening pass; recorded here so nobody rediscovers it as a mystery.
